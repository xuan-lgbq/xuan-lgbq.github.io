Hi, welcome to my website!

I am an undergraduate student at the Jinan University - University of Birmingham Joint Institute, currently working as a research intern at Dartmouth College. I am honored to be advised by Prof. Yaoqing Yang, and I am also currently receiving guidance from Prof. Hongyi Jiang at City University of Hong Kong.

My research interests lie in learning theory, deep learning, and optimization. I am particularly interested in understanding the optimization dynamics of neural networks and sequential decision-making problems, including stochastic gradient descent under structured loss landscapes, Hessian spectral properties, and online routing under hard budget constraints.

01

Research Interests

I am interested in theoretical problems that connect optimization, learning theory, online learning, and modern deep neural networks. My recent work focuses on fine-grained analyses of training dynamics, step-size conditions, Hessian spectral phenomena, and online multi-armed bandit problems with combinatorial structure and budget constraints.

  • Optimization behavior of stochastic gradient descent under ill-conditioned loss landscapes, structured Hessian spectra, and architecture-dependent training dynamics.
  • Theoretically interpretable perspectives on generalization, optimization stability, and neural network training dynamics.
  • Traditional optimization and online learning problems, including combinatorial multi-armed bandits, bandits with knapsacks, and online shortest-path routing.
