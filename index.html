Hi, welcome to my website!

I am an undergraduate student at the Jinan University - University of Birmingham Joint Institute, currently working as a research intern at Dartmouth College. I am honored to be advised by Prof. Yaoqing Yang, and I am also currently receiving guidance from Prof. Hongyi Jiang at City University of Hong Kong.

My research interests lie in learning theory, deep learning, and optimization. I am particularly interested in understanding the optimization dynamics of neural networks, including the behavior of stochastic gradient descent under structured loss landscapes and the spectral properties of Hessian matrices in deep learning.

01

Research Interests

I am interested in theoretical problems that connect optimization, learning theory, and modern deep neural networks. My recent work focuses on fine-grained analyses of training dynamics, step-size conditions, and Hessian spectral phenomena.

  • Optimization behavior of stochastic gradient descent under ill-conditioned landscapes and structured Hessian spectra.
  • Interactions between neural network architecture, loss geometry, and algorithmic choices during training.
  • Theoretically interpretable perspectives on generalization, optimization stability, and neural network training dynamics.
